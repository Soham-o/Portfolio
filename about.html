BIOGRAPHY

Hi, I'm Soham Panda — an AI Automation Engineer and CS student who builds systems that work so people don't have to do things manually.

I got into AI not because of the hype, but because I kept noticing the same pattern everywhere: people spending hours on tasks that follow predictable rules. Internship hunting. Answering the same missed calls. Skimming newsletters for two useful sentences buried in ten paragraphs. Every one of those is a system waiting to be automated.

So I build those systems. My projects aren't demos — they're pipelines that run on free infrastructure, handle real inputs, and deliver real outputs. I obsess over cost constraints, reliability, and designing agents that behave correctly even at 3 AM when nobody is watching. Currently pursuing B.Tech in Computer Science at SGTU while shipping autonomous AI systems on the side.
